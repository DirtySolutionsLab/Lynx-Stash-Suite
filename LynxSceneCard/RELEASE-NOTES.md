## Lynx Scene Card v0.1.9 — Public Preview

This is the first public preview of Lynx Scene Card, a scene-card companion for Stash designed to complement Lynx Theme.

Customize your cards with linked performer and tag chips, portrait previews, compact overflow popups, and an optional studio, date and view-count footer. Additional settings control Stash's thumbnail overlays, progress bar and hover scrubber.

The release includes English settings and documentation, bundled fonts and upstream license information. Lynx Theme is optional.

### Installation

Download the release ZIP and copy the `LynxSceneCard` folder into your Stash plugins directory. Reload plugins, enable Lynx Scene Card and choose the card elements you want under **Settings → Plugins**. Refresh your browser after changing options.

Disable SerechopsSceneCard or other plugins that replace the same card components. See `INSTALLATION.md` for details.

### Preview status

The package has passed syntax, asset and isolated component checks. A fresh installation and live visual/navigation testing have not yet been verified as part of this release review. Please report any layout or compatibility issues.
