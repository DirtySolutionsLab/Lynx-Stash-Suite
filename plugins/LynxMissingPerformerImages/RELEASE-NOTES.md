## Lynx Missing Performer Images v1.2.1 — Public Preview

Developed and maintained by **DirtySolutionsLab with AI assistance**. Distributed under the **MIT License**. See `CREDITS.md` for source history.

This is a Lynx-maintained version of the supplied Missing Performer Images installation, developed further with AI assistance. A separate upstream author has not been verified.

Review performers without portraits, choose a linked library image or use an installed performer scraper, direct image URL or upload, then approve the portrait before saving.

The release includes English guidance, selected-card saving and separate library-only preview/assignment tasks. Google Images is available as a separate-tab shortcut.

### Installation

Copy `LynxMissingPerformerImages` into your Stash plugins directory, reload plugins and refresh your browser. Python is required; no pip packages are needed by the plugin itself.

Keep the `missing_performer_images.yml` manifest filename unchanged and avoid duplicate installed copies. See `INSTALLATION.md`.
